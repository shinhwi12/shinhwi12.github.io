*Void Hymn* is filed as a study in withheld resolution. Piano, close-miked breath, and low electronic pressure circle the same harmonic center without allowing it to become a destination.

The record belongs to the SHINHWI artist catalog rather than a scene-specific Project UNAC soundtrack. Its connection to the archive is atmospheric: a room after transmission, the shape left behind when a signal is removed, and a voice that stays close enough to feel private without becoming confessional.
